# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual and romantic identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behaviour

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment include:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness;
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- taking responsibility, apologising to those affected by mistakes, and learning from the experience;
- focusing on what is best for the project and its community.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, intimidation, discriminatory language, sexualised attention, trolling, sustained disruption, publishing another person's private information, or other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in project spaces and whenever someone is publicly representing the project. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not align with it, and will communicate moderation reasons when appropriate.

Report conduct concerns privately to Davide Lasagna at <davide.lasagna@soton.ac.uk>. Reports will be reviewed promptly, kept confidential as far as practicable, and handled in proportion to the context and impact. Good-faith reporters will not face retaliation.
